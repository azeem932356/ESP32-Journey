\# Day 2 - Circuit Basics



Today I learned the basic concepts needed before working with GPIO.



\- GND (Ground) - the reference point and return path in a circuit.

\- Voltage (V / Volt) - the electrical push that can drive current.

\- Current (A / Ampere) - the flow of electric charge.

\- Resistance (Ohm, Ω) - limits current.

\- LED (Light-Emitting Diode) - produces light when current flows through it in the correct direction.

\- Anode (+) - positive side of an LED.

\- Cathode (-) - negative side of an LED.

\- Circuit - a complete electrical path that allows current to flow.

\- GPIO (General-Purpose Input/Output) - an ESP32 pin that can be controlled by software.

\- HIGH - GPIO is approximately 3.3 V on the ESP32.

\- LOW - GPIO is approximately 0 V.



\## Ohm's Law



V = I × R



V = Voltage

I = Current

R = Resistance



A resistor is used with an LED to limit the current.



\## Basic circuit



ESP32 GPIO -> Resistor -> LED -> GND
